# Number labels audit: game modes outside the academies

Every number taken from a situation or picture should carry a plain-language label right after it, like "40 (total coins) ÷ 20 (equal segments) = 2 (coins per segment)". This register lists each surface in Dice Workshop, Engine City Tycoon, Equation Plaza, Count Lab, Reality Lab, the arcade games, battles and the Notebook, with what changed or why nothing needed to change.
