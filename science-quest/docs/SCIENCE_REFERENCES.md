# Science model references

These sources support underlying principles, not approval of the game or its learning outcomes. The lesson wording, scenarios, numerical exercises and diagrams are original. External science/educator review remains pending.

| Investigation | Principle / source | Deliberate model boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melting Without Warming | [OpenStax Physics, phase change and latent heat](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/11-3-phase-change-and-latent-heat) | Rounded 334 kJ/kg latent heat; ideal equilibrium, no losses, no boiling; fixed 1 kg sample initially at melting point. |
| Separate the Supply Mixture | [OpenStax Chemistry 2e, classification of matter](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/1-2-phases-and-classification-of-matter) | Invented rigid-grain geometry; strict diameter/opening test; no clogging or dissolution model. |
| Same Element, Different Isotope | [OpenStax Chemistry: Atoms First 2e, atomic structure](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-atoms-first-2e/pages/2-3-atomic-structure-and-symbolism) | Counts nucleons for carbon-12/13/14; does not predict stability, decay, abundance or exact mass. |
| Count Atoms Through a Reaction | [OpenStax Chemistry 2e, stoichiometry](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-3-reaction-stoichiometry) | Discrete, complete whole reaction groups; symbolic virtual model only; no rates, activation energy or equilibrium. |
| Account for Bond Energy | [OpenStax Chemistry 2e, bond strengths](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-5-strengths-of-ionic-and-covalent-bonds) | Entirely fictional A–A/B–B/A–B values; approximate broken-minus-formed energy ledger, no real chemical prediction. |

Reference relationships were checked on 2026-09-29. Conservation tests inspect every allowed reaction setting, including odd particle counts and leftover reactants. Diagram output and equivalent accounting tables share the same model results. Whole-course standards alignment has not been established.

## Biology principles added in 1.13

- [OpenStax Biology 2e: overview of photosynthesis](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/8-1-overview-of-photosynthesis): net matter inputs and energy source. The game counts net groups, not intermediate mechanisms or rates.
- [OpenStax Biology 2e: energy and metabolism](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/6-1-energy-and-metabolism): respiration and photosynthesis matter/energy relationships. No ATP yield or pathway kinetics are predicted.
- [OpenStax Biology 2e: the genetic code](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/15-1-the-genetic-code): coding triplets and stop signals. The game uses tiny fictional fragments and does not predict function or organism-level effects.
- [OpenStax Calculus Volume 2: logistic equation](https://openstax.org/books/calculus-volume-2/pages/4-4-the-logistic-equation): continuous logistic solution. Fixed carrying capacity and a closed population are simplifying assumptions.
- [OpenStax Biology 2e: adaptive evolution](https://openstax.org/books/biology-2e/pages/19-3-adaptive-evolution): relative reproductive success. The game models two inherited asexual types without drift, mutation or migration; it does not represent every evolutionary process.

## Earth and space principles added in 1.14

- [National Weather Service: precipitation measurements](https://www.weather.gov/abrfc/map): precipitation gauges and measurement representativeness. The game uses an ideal straight-sided collector and exact volume/area conversion.
- [USGS: understanding plate motions](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/dynamic/understanding.html): rates and geologic intervals. The game explicitly uses full relative separation at a constant rate.
- [USGS: radiometric time scale](https://pubs.usgs.gov/gip/geotime/radiometric.html): parent/daughter relationships and dating interpretation. The game is a dimensionless half-life model without a specific isotope.
- [NASA: solar irradiance science](https://earth.gsfc.nasa.gov/climate/projects/solar-irradiance/science) and [Earth’s energy budget](https://science.nasa.gov/earth/earth-observatory/climate-and-earths-energy-budget/): solar input and global energy balance. The game holds 1361 W/m² fixed and omits atmospheric greenhouse physics.
- [OpenStax Astronomy: electromagnetic spectrum](https://openstax.org/books/astronomy/pages/5-2-the-electromagnetic-spectrum): stellar radiation and area/temperature scaling. The 6000 K reference in the game is fictional, not an asserted solar temperature.
