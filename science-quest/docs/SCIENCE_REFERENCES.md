# Science model references

These sources support underlying principles, not approval of the game or its learning outcomes. The lesson wording, scenarios, numerical exercises and diagrams are original. External science/educator review remains pending.

| Investigation | Principle / source | Deliberate model boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Melting Without Warming | [OpenStax Physics, phase change and latent heat](https://openstax.org/books/physics/pages/11-3-phase-change-and-latent-heat) | Rounded 334 kJ/kg latent heat; ideal equilibrium, no losses, no boiling; fixed 1 kg sample initially at melting point. |
| Separate the Supply Mixture | [OpenStax Chemistry 2e, classification of matter](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/1-2-phases-and-classification-of-matter) | Invented rigid-grain geometry; strict diameter/opening test; no clogging or dissolution model. |
| Same Element, Different Isotope | [OpenStax Chemistry: Atoms First 2e, atomic structure](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-atoms-first-2e/pages/2-3-atomic-structure-and-symbolism) | Counts nucleons for carbon-12/13/14; does not predict stability, decay, abundance or exact mass. |
| Count Atoms Through a Reaction | [OpenStax Chemistry 2e, stoichiometry](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/4-3-reaction-stoichiometry) | Discrete, complete whole reaction groups; symbolic virtual model only; no rates, activation energy or equilibrium. |
| Account for Bond Energy | [OpenStax Chemistry 2e, bond strengths](https://openstax.org/books/chemistry-2e/pages/7-5-strengths-of-ionic-and-covalent-bonds) | Entirely fictional A–A/B–B/A–B values; approximate broken-minus-formed energy ledger, no real chemical prediction. |

Reference relationships were checked on 2026-09-29. Conservation tests inspect every allowed reaction setting, including odd particle counts and leftover reactants. Diagram output and equivalent accounting tables share the same model results. Whole-course standards alignment has not been established.
